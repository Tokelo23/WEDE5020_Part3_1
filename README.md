# Furnimart
## Project Overview
-History
Furnimart is a furniture store that started operating in 2010 and is based in Sandton, South Africa. The store sells top-quality and affordable furniture for homes and offices. We sell products like couches, beds, chairs, mirrors, dining tables, TV stands, TV’s, wardrobes, lamps, office desks and chairs. Furnimart is SA’s no.1 recommended furniture store when it comes to the best and top-notch furniture.
-Mission
Our mission is to create a dream home for our customers because we understand that home is a place where you feel safe and can be your true self. We strive to supply our customers with top quality and affordable furniture.
-vision
We aim to create a safe haven for our customers for them to step into a new and extraordinary world.
-Target Audience
University students who are on a budget and need affordable furniture for their apartments.
Young couples who are ready to move in and start their lives together, looking for stylish and affordable furniture to match their personality.
Young adults who just got their first job and want to move into their own place.
Families with infants who are learning to walk and climb.
Families with teenagers that need tables for doing their homework, drawers for books and pencils and wardrobes for their clothes.
Retirees may need furniture that accommodates their health conditions like a recliner or a lift chair, and light weight furniture that is easier to move around.
People who like to travel would opt to buy furniture that is portable and easy to set up in a new home.
Small businesses would need office furniture like desks and chairs.
## Website Goals and Objectives
The main goal is to give Furnimart an online presence which will make it easier for customers to view available products.

The website will aim to:
Increase the number of sales.
Improve customer experience based on their feedback.
Recommend items based on what customers search for.
Increase visibility and recognition to our target audience.
Increase website traffic to attract potential clients.
Promote all types of furniture from home to office.
Customers will be able to make online orders at the comfort of their homes or office and get their furniture delivered right at their doorstep.
Show furniture products and prices.
Promote the brand and products.


KPIs: 
Increase the number of customers visiting the store.
Increase website visitors by 40% within 2 months.
Increase online enquiries by 20%.
Increase product views by 30%.
Receive at least 50 online orders every month.
## Key Features and Functionality
The website will have 6 main pages:
1.Home page: Hero shot image, a short welcome message, featured products and promotions.
2. About Us: Information about the store, history, vision and mission statements.
3. Products: Categories of furniture, images, product names and prices.
4. Services: 
Delivery services offered by Furnimart.
Assembly: Helping customers to assemble their furniture after delivery.
Customization: Customers can enquire about how they would like their furniture to be, like the colour, design and preferred size. 
Customers can get advice on choosing furniture that matches their room.
Bulk orders: Businesses and companies can enquire about buying furniture in bulk.
5.Enquiry: It will consist of a form where customers will fill in their details and will be able to ask about the products and services we offer. The form will include name, email, phone number and message.
6.Contact Us: It will contain the store’s details like phone number, email address, physical address, business hours and social media links.
